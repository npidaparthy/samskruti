॥ atha aṣṭaṣaṣṭitamaṃ daśakam – 68  gōpikānāmāhlādaprakaṭanam ॥
॥ chandaḥ – sammatā ॥
tava vilōkanāt kṛṣṇa gōpikājanāḥ
pramadasaṅkulāḥ kṛṣṇa paṅkajēkṣaṇa ।
amṛtadhārayā kṛṣṇa samplutā iva 
stimitatāṃ dadhuḥ kṛṣṇa tvatpurōgatāḥ ॥1॥

tadanu kācana kṛṣṇa tvatkarāmbujaṃ 
sapadi gṛhṇatī kṛṣṇa nirviśaṅkitam ।
ghanapayōdharē kṛṣṇa saṃnnidhāya sā 
pulakasaṃvṛtā kṛṣṇa tasthuṣī ciram ॥2॥

tava vibhō'parā kṛṣṇa kōmalaṃ bhujaṃ 
nijagalāntarē kṛṣṇa paryavēṣṭayat ।
galasamudgataṃ kṛṣṇa prāṇamārutaṃ 
pratinirundhatīva kṛṣṇa atiharṣulā ॥3॥

apagatatrapā kṛṣṇa kāpi kāminī 
tava mukhāmbujāt kṛṣṇa pūgacarvitam ।
pratigṛhayya tat kṛṣṇa vaktrapaṅkajē 
nidadhatī gatā kṛṣṇa pūrṇakāmatām ॥4॥

vikaruṇō vanē kṛṣṇa saṃvihāya mām
apagatō'si kā kṛṣṇa tvāmiha spṛśēt ।
iti sarōṣayā kṛṣṇa tāvadēkayā 
sajalalōcanaṃ kṛṣṇa vīkṣitō bhavān ॥5॥

iti mudā''kulaiḥ kṛṣṇa vallavījanaiḥ 
samamupāgataḥ kṛṣṇa yāmunē taṭē ।
mṛdukucāmbaraiḥ kṛṣṇa kalpitāsanē 
ghusṛṇabhāsurē kṛṣṇa paryaśōbhathāḥ ॥6॥

katividhā kṛpā kṛṣṇa kē'pi sarvataḥ
dhṛtadayōdayāḥ kṛṣṇa kēcidāśritē ।
katicidīdṛśāḥ kṛṣṇa mādṛśēṣvapi
ityabhihitō bhavān kṛṣṇa vallavījanaiḥ ॥7॥

ayi kumārikāḥ kṛṣṇa naiva śaṅkyatāṃ 
kaṭhinatā mayi kṛṣṇa prēmakātarē ।
mayi tu cētasaḥ kṛṣṇa vō'nuvṛttayē 
kṛtamidaṃ mayēti kṛṣṇa ucivānbhavān ॥8॥

ayi niśamyatāṃ kṛṣṇa jīvavallabhāḥ 
priyatamō janaḥ kṛṣṇa nēdṛśō mama ।
tadiha ramyatāṃ kṛṣṇa ramyayāminī
ṣvanuparōdhamiti kṛṣṇa ālapō vibhō ॥9॥

iti girādhikaṃ kṛṣṇa mōdamēduraiḥ
rvrajavadhūjanaiḥ kṛṣṇa sākamāraman ।
kalitakautukaḥ kṛṣṇa rāsakhēlanē 
gurupurīpatē kṛṣṇa pāhi māṃ gadāt ॥10॥

॥ iti śrīmannārāyaṇīyē gōpikānāmāhlādaprakaṭanaṃ nāma aṣṭaṣaṣṭitamaṃ daśakaṃ samāptam ॥
