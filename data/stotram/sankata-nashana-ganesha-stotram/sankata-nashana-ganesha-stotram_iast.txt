॥ saṅkaṭanāśagaṇēśastōtram ॥

॥ nārada uvāca ॥
praṇamya śirasā dēvaṃ gaurīputraṃ vināyakam ।
bhaktāvāsaṃ smarēnnityamāyuḥ kāmārthasiddhayē ॥ 1 ॥

prathamaṃ vakratuṇḍaṃ ca ēkadantaṃ dvitīyakam ।
tṛtīyaṃ kṛṣṇapiṅgākṣaṃ gajavaktraṃ caturthakam ॥ 2 ॥

lambōdaraṃ pañcamaṃ ca ṣaṣṭhaṃ vikaṭamēva ca ।
saptamaṃ vighnarājaṃ ca dhūmravarṇaṃ tathāṣṭamam ॥ 3 ॥

navamaṃ phālacandraṃ ca daśamaṃ tu vināyakam ।
ēkādaśaṃ gaṇapatiṃ dvādaśaṃ tu gajānanam ॥ 4 ॥

dvādaśaitāni nāmāni trisandhyaṃ yaḥ paṭhēnnaraḥ ।
na ca vighnabhayaṃ tasya sarvasiddhikaraṃ param ॥ 5 ॥

vidyārthī labhatē vidyāṃ dhanārthī labhatē dhanam ।
putrārthī labhatē putrānmōkṣārthī labhatē gatim ॥ 6 ॥

japēdgaṇapatistōtraṃ ṣaḍbhirmāsaiḥ phalaṃ labhēt ।
saṃvatsarēṇa siddhiṃ ca labhatē nātra saṃśayaḥ ॥ 7 ॥

aṣṭabhyō brāhmaṇēbhyaśca likhitvā yaḥ samarpayēt ।
tasya vidyā bhavētsarvā gaṇēśasya prasādataḥ ॥ 8 ॥

॥ śrī gaṇēśāya namaḥ ॥
॥ iti śrīnāradapurāṇē 
saṅkaṭanāśagaṇēśastōtraṃ sampūrṇam ॥
